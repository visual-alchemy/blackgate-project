## Key Decisions & System Constraints Resolved
- **Audio Playout Clocking**: Disabling sync on `decklinkaudiosink` prevented GStreamer's master clock-slaving mechanism from slaving to the hardware audio playback clock. Over time, the clock skew between the system clock and the DeckLink hardware clock led to buffer underruns, producing the audio stuttering/dropouts. Enabling `sync=true` on the audio sink allows GStreamer to dynamically align playout to the system clock while the video sink is kept synced upstream.
