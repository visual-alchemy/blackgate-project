- Analyzed 10-second raw PCM audio samples from BWF feeds on remote machine `192.168.40.61` using `astats` and python.
- Identified massive constant DC offset (bias) in the incoming RTMP streams:
  - Court 1: Mean (DC) `2606.4` (~8% scale, -22 dBFS bias)
  - Court 2: Mean (DC) `3385.69` (~10% scale, -20 dBFS bias)
- Measured extremely low AC signal (atmosphere) levels:
  - Court 1: AC RMS `-50.78 dB`
  - Court 2: AC RMS `-40.41 dB`
- Formulated analysis:
  - VLC/Wowza/browsers apply high-pass filtering (block DC) and digital gain automatically.
  - SDI/DeckLink card passes raw PCM directly. Professional switchers mute channels with high DC offsets or threshold noise.
  - Corrective action: Fix venue encoder (ground loop & input gain) or update gateway codebase with configurable highpass/gain.
